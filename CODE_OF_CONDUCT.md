# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation to be respectful, technically honest, and safe. Be
constructive, critique ideas rather than people, state uncertainty, protect
private data, and make room for people with different backgrounds and levels
of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
misrepresentation, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information
without permission are unacceptable in project spaces or when representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through a maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not
include sensitive personal details in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions, pause participation, or permanently exclude a participant
when needed to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be
proportionate, documented privately, and avoid conflicts of interest where
possible.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and project-related communication.
