## Synthetic FIT fixture

The synthetic FIT activity is created deterministically at test runtime by
Workout Tracker code. It uses invented coordinates near 1 degree latitude and
longitude, an invented 2024 timestamp, and no manufacturer, product, serial,
provider account, credential, or real activity input. No FIT binary is checked
in. The generated bytes are original test data distributed under the
repository's MIT License.

Maintainers may optionally supply a private FIT through
`WORKOUT_REAL_FIT_PATH`. That file is read locally, is not copied into the
repository, is ignored by Git under `tests/fixtures/private/`, and is never
uploaded or printed by the compatibility test.

## Public visual and audio assets

- PNG icons (`favicon.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, and `icon-*.png`) are
  generated from original Workout Tracker geometry by
  `scripts/generate-public-assets.mjs`.
- WAV cues under `public/audio/` are generated from original deterministic
  triangle-wave sequences by the same script. They contain no recorded voice,
  sample, or third-party sound.

The generated PNG icons and WAV cues are distributed under the repository's
MIT License. Run `npm run assets:check` to verify that every redistributed
binary asset exactly matches its documented generator.
